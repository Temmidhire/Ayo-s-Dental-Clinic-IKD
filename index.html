ABOUT THE PRACTICE

A family practice with a personal approach.

Family Dental Clinic is a family-owned dental practice in Ikorodu, Lagos, built around accessible, respectful and patient-centred dental care.

Our goal is to make dental visits clearer and more comfortable while helping patients maintain healthy smiles over the long term.
